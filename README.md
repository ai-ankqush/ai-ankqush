# Ankush Chowdhary

**Cybersecurity & AI-security executive — CISO-calibre operator and the architect of how enterprises govern AI.**

Founder & CEO of [Neo Control](https://neocontrol.ai) · Founder & Managing Partner of [Keystone Executive](https://www.keystoneexecutive.ai) · Creator of the [AI Control Architecture](https://aicontrolarchitecture.org).

I work at the point where executive accountability meets the practical governance, runtime control and assurance of enterprise AI — authorizing the *action*, not just the actor.

---

### What I build and why

Most AI security stops at the model or the login. The consequential question is different: **what is the AI allowed to *do*** — and can you take it back. That question is what I call **control sovereignty**, and it runs through everything below.

- **[AI Control Architecture (ACA)](https://aicontrolarchitecture.org)** — the open framework for governing what AI systems are allowed to see, decide and do. A 5-tier risk model, 10 control pillars, pattern-based control selection, and evidence/assurance crosswalks to NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, the EU AI Act and OWASP LLM/Agentic. Licensed **CC BY 4.0**.
- **[AI Action Fabric](https://github.com/ai-ankqush/neo-community-edition)** — the open runtime that *enforces* ACA. Every consequential agent action becomes a fresh, scoped, revocable delegation, evaluated at execution time against its purpose, blast radius and reversibility. Shadow-first. Licensed **AGPL-3.0**.
- **Recursive Cyber Defense Model (RCDM)** — a model for governed, machine-speed cyber defense in the era of autonomous adversaries.
